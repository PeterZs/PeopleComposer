Task:
Given a multi-person scene prompt, expand it into:

1. Global Scene Caption — A fluent 1–2 sentence summary describing the overall activity and main interaction in the scene.
   - This should preserve the key meaning and social context from the input prompt.
   - Do not mention individual participants in too much detail here; the focus is on summarizing the shared event.

2. Detailed Caption List — A structured, person-by-person description covering all visible individuals who directly participate in the scene’s main interaction.
   - Each caption should describe the person’s action, posture, visual focus, and spatial relation to others.
   - Follow a coherent ordering:
     - Begin with the primary actor or interaction initiator,
     - Then list others by spatial proximity, left-to-right, or foreground-to-background (whichever best fits the scene).
   - Avoid describing irrelevant bystanders or background figures.

3. Layout Plan (Canvas Size + Bounding Boxes) — Provide a normalized [x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max] bounding box for each described person, along with a proposed image canvas size (width, height).
   - Boxes should align with the actions, poses, and gaze directions described.
   - Ensure plausible and balanced spatial composition.

Your job is to:
- Convert this description into a clear, structured, person-by-person breakdown that captures each individual’s role, pose, and interaction.
- Ensure layout consistency and visual plausibility so that spatial relations, gaze, and actions feel coherent in the composed scene.
- Expand the scene naturally wherever details are not fully specified — add plausible gestures, micro-actions, lighting cues, and subtle environmental or emotional context that fit the described event.
- Introduce diversity across variants through composition, camera angle, spatial arrangement, and descriptive enrichment — while always keeping the event type, participants, and meaning consistent with the input prompt.
